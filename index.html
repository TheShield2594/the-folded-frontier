How to play

A D
Walk. Space or W to jump, S to drop through platforms
Left click
Use the held item: mine, swing, place blocks
Right click
Open doors and chests, talk to the Merchant, set spawn at a bed
1–0 / wheel
Pick a hotbar slot
E
Backpack, gear and crafting. Shift-click moves items fast. The tabs on top turn to your Party, the Bestiary (every enemy you've defeated and what they dropped), the Museum and the Town
Q · R
Switch partner · Use your partner's special move
C
Hold to flatten: crawl through one-tile gaps and dodge hits
Shift
Dash. You can't be hit mid-dash, and you get one dash in the air per jump. The ring at your feet shows the cooldown
X / hold Right click
Block with a shield worn in an accessory slot. Raise it just as a hit lands to parry: no damage, the attacker is staggered and projectiles fly back
Bows & hammers
Hold to draw a bow and let go to shoot; a full draw (the ring above you turns gold) always crits. Warhammers swing slowly but knock enemies flying and cancel their wind-ups
Fire · Ink · Water
Some weapons carry a damage type. Fire burns over time, ink slows, water soaks (softer hits taken and dealt by the target) and puts out fire. Enemies take more from what they're weak to; the Bestiary lists it
Fishing
Hold a fishing rod with bait in your backpack and click to cast into a pool of ink. When the bobber dips and a ! pops up, click to reel it in. Better rods and bait catch rarer fish; the Emberite Rod can fish in lava
Pets & mounts
Use a pet item to have it follow you (use it again to send it home). Use a mount item, or press G, to ride it: faster running and higher jumps. Flattening, climbing or firing the hook gets you down
F
Fire the Grappling Hook. On a rope, hold W or S to climb
H · M · Esc
Drink a healing potion · World map · Pause and save
Touch
On a phone or tablet, the stick on the left walks (push up or down to climb). Hold Use to use the held item and drag off it to aim, or touch the world to use it right there. Tap a townsperson, door or chest to talk or open it, or press Talk near one. In the backpack, tap to pick up and put down, hold to split a stack, and turn on Quick move to send items straight across
Gamepad
Plug one in and press a button. X dashes, RT uses the held item (hold it to draw a bow) and holding B blocks. Keys and buttons can be changed in Settings.
NICE!
Stomp enemies by landing on them and tap Space on impact. With a sword, click again when the blade flashes gold near the end of a swing for a bonus hit worth 1.8× damage. Clicking too early spoils that swing's chance.
