The Folded Frontier: armour parts sheets, one per metal.

How to run each one:
- Model: Nano Banana Pro, wide aspect ratio (16:9) at 2K.
- Attach player-parts-reference.png as the reference image.
- For consistent scale and style across sets: once you have a good first set, attach it as a second reference for the others.
- Save each result with the filename shown and upload to the branch claude/higgsfield-account-setup-qessqc in a renders/ folder.

======================================================================
COPPER  (save as renders/armor-cu.png)
======================================================================
Armour parts sheet for 2D cut-out (paper doll) animation, for the exact character in the reference image. Keep the same art style, the same face, hair and colours, the same thick dark plum-black ink outlines and white paper border, and draw every piece at the SAME SCALE as the matching piece in the reference. Everything in strict SIDE VIEW facing right.

Lay out these four separate pieces in a single row with generous empty space between them, none touching or overlapping:
(1) the same head in side profile facing right, wearing a simple rounded copper kettle cap with a small flat brim and a row of rivets; the helmet fits the head snugly, covers the top and back of the head and the hair, and leaves the eye, cheek, blush and smile fully visible; short neck stub below, like the reference head;
(2) the torso only, wearing a copper ring-mail shirt over the teal tunic, held with brown leather straps, keeping the red scarf wrapped at the neck with its tail trailing behind to the left and the brown satchel strap and tan pouch; NO arms, NO legs, NO head; same neck stub on top as the reference torso;
(3) one arm wearing a copper ring-mail sleeve with a brown leather bracer, peach hand, hanging straight down, rounded shoulder top;
(4) one leg wearing a copper knee plate strapped over the dark purple trouser leg and a copper toe cap on the brown boot, straight, toe pointing right, rounded hip top.

Metal colours: warm copper orange-brown (#c8743a) with darker folds (#8a4a24) and a light peach highlight. Simple soft shading and one highlight stroke on the upper left, no glossy 3D rendering. Each piece is its own paper cut-out with its own white border. Plain flat light grey background, no text, no labels, no shadow, no ground.
