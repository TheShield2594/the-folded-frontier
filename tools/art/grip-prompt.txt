======================================================================
GRIPPING ARMS  (save as renders/grip-arms.png)
======================================================================
Sheet of 7 separate arm pieces for 2D cut-out (paper doll) animation, for the exact character in the reference images, same art style, same thick dark plum-black ink outlines (#2a2130), same white paper border, same scale as the arm in the reference. Each arm hangs STRAIGHT DOWN, rounded shoulder at the top, in strict side view, and ends in a CLOSED FIST gripping a round handle that is NOT drawn: the fingers curl around an empty round hole running through the fist from front to back, knuckles facing right, thumb on top.

Lay out the 7 arms in a single row left to right in this order, all the same size, with generous empty space between them, none touching or overlapping:
1. plain teal tunic sleeve (as in the reference arm)
2. copper armour sleeve: copper ring mail and brown leather bracer
3. iron armour sleeve: iron chainmail and plain iron vambrace
4. gold armour sleeve: gold scales and engraved gold vambrace
5. frostsilver armour sleeve: frostsilver chainmail and vambrace with a small ice crystal
6. inkstone armour sleeve: dark inkstone plate with ink drips and a glowing purple line
7. emberite armour sleeve: charcoal plate with glowing ember cracks and a small flame at the wrist

Peach hand and fist on every arm. Each piece is its own paper cut-out with a complete ink outline and its own white border. Plain flat light grey background, no weapon, no handle, no text, no labels, no shadow.
