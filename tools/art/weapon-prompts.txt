The Folded Frontier: weapon and tool icon sheets. These are the item icons, which the game also uses as the weapon in the player's hand.

How to run each one:
- Model: Nano Banana Pro, 2K. Use 16:9 for the 2-row sheets and 21:9 or 16:9 for the shields.
- Attach two references: player-parts-reference.png (style) and weapon-icons-now.jpg (the current icons, for layout and orientation).
- Once you have one good sheet, attach it as an extra reference for the others so they match.
- Paste each result here in its own message, or upload it to the branch in renders/ with the filename shown.

SWORDS  (save as renders/weapons-swords.png)
======================================================================
Swords icon sheet for a 2D papercraft game, matching the art style of the reference image exactly (Paper Mario-like paper cut-outs, thick dark plum-black ink outlines #2a2130, white paper cut-out border, subtle paper grain, flat soft colours with one highlight stroke on the upper left, chunky rounded shapes, no glossy 3D rendering).

Draw exactly 8 separate items in a neat grid of 2 rows and 4 columns, reading left to right then top to bottom in this order, all the same size, each centred in its own grid cell with generous empty space around it, none touching or overlapping:
1. Wooden Sword: a simple carved wooden practice sword, light wood blade, darker wood grip
2. Copper Sword: short copper blade (#c8743a) with rivets on the crossguard, leather-wrapped grip
3. Iron Sword: straight grey iron blade (#8f98a3), plain crossguard, leather grip
4. Gold Broadsword: wide gold blade (#f1c04f) with an engraved swirl near the guard, ornate crossguard, red gem in the pommel
5. Frostsilver Blade: pale icy silver-blue blade (#cfe3ee) with ice crystals growing from the crossguard
6. Ink Cutlass: curved dark violet-black cutlass (#3a2d4a) with ink drips along the edge and a glowing purple line (#b58cf0)
7. Emberite Greatsword: large charcoal blade (#3b3034) with glowing ember-orange cracks (#ff7a2d) and a flame-shaped crossguard
8. The Unfolded Edge: a legendary sword folded from cream paper (#f4f0e6) like origami, crisp fold creases and ink-drawn edges, a small ink-blot gem in the guard

Orientation: every item lies diagonally at 45 degrees with the handle or grip at the bottom-left and the blade, head or tip at the top-right, like the icons in the second reference image.

Each item is its own paper cut-out with a complete ink outline and its own white border. Plain flat light grey background, no text, no labels, no numbers, no shadow, no ground.
